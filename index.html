YOUR PERSONAL WORKOUT GUIDE ✨

Harley's Fitness Journey 💜

One day at a time. Choose a day, follow each exercise, and tick it off when you're done.

Your weekly workouts

All workouts are listed below. You can read them even if the interactive buttons do not load.

Monday — Treadmill + Glutes

  • Treadmill: 30 min — 5 min easy, 20 min comfortable, 5 min easy.
  • Chair squats: 2 × 10 — Lower onto a sturdy chair and stand back up.
  • Glute kickbacks: 2 × 10 each leg — Hold a counter, move leg backwards slowly.

Tuesday — Upper Body + Core

  • Wall push-ups: 2 × 10–12 — Bend elbows towards wall, push back.
  • Band rows: 2 × 12 — Secure band around feet, pull elbows towards ribs.
  • Shoulder press: 2 × 10 — Press light weights up, then lower.
  • Standing knee raises: 2 × 10 each — Hold counter, lift knee gently.

Wednesday — Gym

  • Warm-up: 7 min gentle walk or cycling.
  • Leg press: 2 × 10–12 — Push plate away, slowly return.
  • Hip abduction: 2 × 12–15 — Move knees out and return.
  • Seated leg curl: 2 × 10–12 — Bend knees against pad.
  • Chest press: 2 × 10 — Push handles forward and return.
  • Seated row: 2 × 10–12 — Pull handles to ribs and return.
  • Cooldown: 5–10 min easy walking.

Thursday — Recovery

  • Optional easy walk or stretching: 15–20 min. Rest if tired.

Friday — Treadmill + Glutes

  • Treadmill: 25–30 min at comfortable pace.
  • Chair squats: 2 × 10–12 — Sit slowly and stand.
  • Glute bridges: 2 × 10–12 — Lie on back, knees bent, lift hips and lower.
  • Side leg raises: 2 × 10 each — Hold counter, raise leg sideways.

Saturday — Optional

  • 20–30 min gentle family walk, stretching or rest.

Sunday — Rest

  • Rest and recover. 💜

💜 Go at your own pace. Start with light resistance, rest when needed, and stop if you feel pain, dizzy or unusually breathless. Ask a gym instructor to set up the machines on Wednesday.
